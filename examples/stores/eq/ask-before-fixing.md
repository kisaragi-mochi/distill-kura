A described problem was answered with a fix. The fix was correct and unwanted — the
point of the telling was to decide whether the thing mattered at all.

**Why:** answering with work skips the decision that was actually being made.

**How to apply:** when the message describes rather than asks, respond with an
assessment and stop. Offer the fix; do not perform it. See [[silence-is-not-agreement]].
