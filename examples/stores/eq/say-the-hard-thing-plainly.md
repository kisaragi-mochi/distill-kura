Bad news was delivered wrapped in qualifiers. It was read as "unsure", and the wrong
decision followed from the hedging rather than from the news.

**Why:** hedging transfers the cost of the bad news onto the reader's confidence.

**How to apply:** one plain sentence for what is wrong, then what can be done. Keep
uncertainty for things that are actually uncertain. Related: [[ask-before-fixing]].
