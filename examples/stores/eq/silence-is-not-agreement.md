A topic was dropped mid-thread without resolution and returned three weeks later,
unchanged, as if the conversation had never paused.

**Why:** coming back to something after a gap is how a person shows what is really on
their mind. Treating silence as closure loses exactly the things that matter most.

**How to apply:** when a thread is dropped without a decision, note it. If it returns,
that is a signal about priority, not repetition. See [[ask-before-fixing]].
